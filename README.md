# Click Wisely!
## Introduction
Click Wisely! is a 3D educational game about keeping yourself safe and secure when encountered by possible real-world cybersecurity threats! From confidential test scores to coffee meet ups, the player has to avoid getting compromised by unsafe choices. By exploring the office, the player will encounter multiple risks, where they have to choose between options to make the safest decision. Based on these decisions, the final challenge could become way more challenging or very easy—there are Cyber Clues given based on how many safe options the player chose. Through this gamified experience, the player can gain relevant information on the measures that need to be taken when encountering physical cybersecurity threats.  

## Inspiration
We believe not all cybersecurity threats come through suspicious emails and online scams. However, digital threats are the primary focus of cybersecurity education. Because of this, there is less attention and awareness education for physical cybersecurity threats, which are still very dangerous. We created this app to bring to attention the threats that physical items can pose, and teach people how they should be handled.  

## Features
- Simulated office with decorations (tables, drawers, plants, windows)
- Risk items are put around the office (3 different types of USB, 2 different types of poster)
- Dialogue Manager pop up when risk items approached with questions about cybersecurity risks
- Holes on the floor turned visible after the player finishes the 5th risk item. Only one hole is the correct exit path. The number of holes depend on correct decisions made throughout the game.
- WASD and up/down/left/right arrow and space keys to move back/forth and turn left/right
